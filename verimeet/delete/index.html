Delete your account

VeriMeet by Dangeon Studio

In the app

  1. Open VeriMeet and go to Profile, then Account and safety.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account.

Without the app

Email hello@dangeonstudio.com from the email address on your account (or the one on your Google or Apple sign-in) with the subject "Delete my VeriMeet account". We will confirm it is you and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, questionnaire answers, neighborhood, matches, date chats and notification settings.
  • Any upcoming dates are cancelled; the other people are told only that you can't make it.
  • Our ID partner Didit is told to delete your ID images, selfie and face data.

What is kept

  • If someone reported you or you were banned: a small safety record (first name, birth date, number of reports, report reasons, and whether you were banned), so banned members cannot sign up again.
  • Purchase records, as long as tax law requires.
  • Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Premium subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store. Cancel yours there too so you are not charged again.
