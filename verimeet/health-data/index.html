Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026 · Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy is for "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada, Connecticut and other states. VeriMeet is run by Daniil Gerasimov, an individual doing business as Dangeon Studio ("we" or "us").

1. What we collect, and why

Consumer health dataWhy we collect it and how we use it
Biometric data: face data created from your ID photo, your live selfie and your main profile photo during the ID check and the main photo face matchTo confirm that you are a real person who matches your ID, and that your main profile photo shows you. Never used for anything else.
Food allergies and diet needs that may relate to your health, such as gluten-free or a nut or shellfish allergyTo pick restaurants and meeting places that are safe for you. Never shown to your matches.
Answers about drinking alcohol and smoking or vaping, if you choose to give themTo match you with people whose habits fit what matters to you. Never shown to your matches.

We do not collect precise location, and we do not collect information about health conditions, treatment, medication or health care services.

2. Where it comes from

  • From you: your answers in the app, and the photos and selfie you take.
  • From Didit, our ID check provider: the face comparisons it makes from your ID photo, selfie and main photo. VeriMeet receives only the result, such as "matched".

3. Your consent

We collect biometric data only after you agree in the app, and we collect the other data only when you choose to give it, to provide the matching and meeting places you ask for. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 6).

4. Consumer health data we share

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it with third parties or affiliates. We have no affiliates.

Our processors handle it only on our behalf, under contracts that limit its use:

  • Didit performs the ID check and face match (biometric data).
  • Supabase stores your answers (servers in the U.S.).
  • GitHub runs our weekly matching job, which reads the answers needed to match you and pick places.

We would disclose consumer health data to anyone else only if the law requires it, and we will update this policy and ask for your consent before any other sharing.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data;
  • access your consumer health data, including a list of all third parties and affiliates it was shared with, and how to contact them (today, there are none);
  • withdraw consent to our collection and sharing of it; and
  • delete it. We also tell our processors to delete it, including from archives and backups.

We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

6. How to use your rights

  • In the app: withdraw face data consent under Profile, Account and safety, Face check. Change your food and values answers at any time, or email us to delete them. Delete your account under Profile, Account and safety, which deletes all of this data.
  • By email: write to hello@dangeonstudio.com with "Health data request" in the subject. We confirm it is you by replying to the email on your account.

We answer within 45 days. If we need more time, we will tell you, and we may take up to 45 more days. Requests are free up to twice a year.

Appeals. If we refuse your request, reply with "Appeal" within 45 days of our answer. We will answer within 45 days and explain our decision. If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint, or your own state's attorney general.

7. Contact

Email hello@dangeonstudio.com. Our full Privacy Policy covers everything else we collect.
