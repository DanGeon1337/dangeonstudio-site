1. Who we are

VeriMeet is an app that matches verified adults into one-on-one dates or small friend groups that meet in public places. It is run by Daniil Gerasimov, an individual doing business as Dangeon Studio, called "we" or "us" here. VeriMeet is offered only in the United States. This policy covers the VeriMeet app and the VeriMeet pages at dangeonstudio.com.

3. ID and registry checks

Every member must pass an ID check before their city opens for matching. Here is exactly what happens:

  1. ID and selfie. After you agree in the app, you photograph your government ID and take a live selfie inside a check run by our partner Didit. Didit confirms the ID looks genuine, that you are 18 or older, and that your face matches the ID. This uses face data, a type of biometric data (see section 4).
  2. What we receive. Whether you passed, a reason code if you did not, and, if you passed, your first name, last name and birth date. If you do not pass, we keep no personal details from the check, only the result and the reason code.
  3. Sex offender registry check. A person on our team searches your first and last name on the U.S. Department of Justice's Dru Sjodin National Sex Offender Public Website (NSOPW) and compares any results with your birth date. This is the only background check we run. We do not check criminal records, credit or anything else. We use registry information only to protect members, who meet people in person, from the risk of sexual offenses, and never for any other purpose. If a result is unclear, we review it by hand, and you can ask us to look again. If we ever add another check, we will update this policy and ask you first.
  4. Last name deleted. As soon as the registry check has a result, your last name is deleted automatically.
  5. Main photo face match. Your first profile photo must clearly show your face. Didit compares it with your ID check selfie. Our server passes the two images to Didit for each comparison without saving them, and we keep only the result.
  6. Photo moderation. All photos are scanned by Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch for nudity and violent content, and may be reviewed by our team.

Didit handles your ID images and selfie as our service provider. Its verification privacy notice explains how it protects them.

Please note: no check is perfect. Passing these checks does not mean a member is safe or truthful. Read our Safety and community standards.

4. Face data policy

This section is our public written policy for biometric data, as required by laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, Texas and Washington biometric laws, and the Colorado Privacy Act.

  • What it is. The face data Didit creates from your ID photo, your live selfie and your main profile photo in order to compare them. VeriMeet itself does not create, keep or store face scans, face templates or your selfie.
  • Why we use it. Only to confirm that you are a real person who matches your ID, and that your main profile photo shows you. Never for anything else.
  • Consent. We collect it only after you agree in writing in the app, and you can withdraw consent at any time under Profile, Account and safety, Face check.
  • No selling. We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from face data. We disclose it only to Didit, which processes it for us under contract, or when the law requires it.
  • How long it is kept. Didit keeps your ID images and selfie while your account is open, so a new main photo can be checked against your selfie. We have them permanently destroyed at the earliest of:
    • right away when you delete your account or withdraw consent;
    • 30 days after an ID check you did not pass, or after the registry check did not clear you, unless you asked us to review it;
    • 30 days after a newer ID check replaces an older one (for the older one);
    • 12 months after you last used VeriMeet. Your face check then ends, and you would verify again before your next match.
    In every case, this happens before the deadline any law sets, including within 3 years of your last interaction with us and within 1 year after the purpose ends.
  • Protection and incidents. Face data is held by Didit with encryption and strict access controls. If we learn of a security incident that may affect face data, we will investigate and contain it, and notify affected members and authorities as the law requires, without unreasonable delay.

5. How we use your information

  • To create your account, verify your identity and age, and run the registry check.
  • To match you with other members each week and pick public places to meet, including places that fit everyone's food needs and budget, and indoor places when bad weather is forecast.
  • To run date plans, the date chat, check-ins, notifications and emails (such as sign-in codes and safety messages).
  • To keep members safe: review reports, enforce attendance rules and pauses, remove people who break our rules, and tell members when someone they talked to was banned for fraud.
  • To process purchases, Premium and invite rewards.
  • To fix bugs, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal duties.

Matching is automatic and decides only who we suggest you meet and where. We do not use your information for targeted advertising or for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We may create statistics that cannot identify you, such as how many members a city has, and we will not try to re-identify them.

6. What other members see

There is no browsing of profiles. Only the people you are matched with see your match card: your first name, age, photos, your city or neighborhood and a rough distance, shared interests, and that your ID and photo are verified. After a date is set, they also see your check-in status and the messages in that date's chat. They never see your last name, birth date, email, exact location, ID details, food needs or values answers. Feedback and reports go only to VeriMeet, and the person is never told who reported them.

If you use Share my plan, the details go only to the people you choose, through your phone's own messaging apps. VeriMeet does not see who you send it to.

If we ban a member for fraud or a scam, everyone who was in a date chat with them gets a safety notice with their first name, when they last wrote, and that they were banned because we believe they may have used a false identity or tried to get money through fraud. The law requires this notice.

8. How long we keep it

  • Last name: until the registry check has a result, then deleted automatically.
  • Failed ID check: only the result and reason code, with no personal details.
  • Face data at Didit: as described in section 4.
  • Date chat: the chat closes one day after the date, unless you both want to meet again. Messages are kept with your account so reports can be reviewed, and are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Everything else: while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, answers, messages and other data, and have Didit delete your ID check. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Safety record: if someone reported you or you were banned, we keep a small record after deletion (first name, birth date, number of reports, report reasons and whether you were banned), for as long as it is needed to stop banned members from signing up again.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting laws require.

9. Your choices and rights

Every member has these rights, whatever state you live in:

  • Know and access: get a copy of your data in the app under Profile, Account and safety, or ask us what we collect, use and disclose about you.
  • Correct: edit your answers, photos, neighborhood and modes at any time, or ask us to fix anything else.
  • Delete: delete your account in the app under Profile, Account and safety, or without the app.
  • Withdraw consent to face data use under Profile, Account and safety, Face check. The ID check is required for matching, so withdrawing pauses matching until you check again.
  • Opt out of any sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling. We do none of these, but you can still ask, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals.
  • Turn off location access and notifications in your phone's settings.

How to ask. Email hello@dangeonstudio.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We confirm it is you by replying to the email address on your account. Someone you authorize in writing can ask for you. We answer within 45 days and will tell you if we need up to 45 more. Requests are free.

Appeals. If we refuse any part of your request, reply with "Appeal" within 45 days and we will answer within 45 days. If you are still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general.

We will never treat you differently, or charge you a different price, for using these rights.

11. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit and at rest, access rules that let each member read only their own data, private photo storage, and access for only a small number of staff accounts. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Age limit

VeriMeet is only for people 18 and older. The ID check confirms age, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.
