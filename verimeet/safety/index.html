1. What we check, and what we don't

VERIMEET DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND CHECKS ON ITS MEMBERS.

Before a member can be matched or message anyone, VeriMeet checks their government ID and a live selfie through our partner Didit, and a person on our team searches their name on the U.S. Department of Justice's National Sex Offender Public Website.

These checks have limits:

  • They are not foolproof, and they are not a perfect safety solution. They can give a false sense of security.
  • People who mean harm can try to get around them, for example with stolen or fake documents.
  • Many people who have committed crimes are not on any registry, and registries can be incomplete or out of date.
  • A name search can miss someone, or match the wrong person.
  • We check members when they join, and again only if someone reports them.

2. Online dating safety awareness

  • Anyone who is able to commit identity theft can also fake a dating profile, even with our checks.
  • There is no substitute for acting with caution when communicating with any stranger who wants to meet you.
  • Keep your last name, email address, home address, phone number, workplace and other identifying details private until you know someone well. VeriMeet never shows your last name or address to matches.
  • Stop talking to anyone who pressures you for personal or financial information, or tries to trick you into revealing it.
  • Meet only at the public place in your plan, with plenty of people around. Never agree to be picked up at home. Get to and from the date on your own.
  • Always tell a friend or family member where you are going and when you will be back. Use Share my plan in the app to send them the details.
  • Stay in control. Watch your drink, keep your phone charged, and keep your belongings with you.
  • Trust your instincts. You can leave at any time, and cancelling because you feel unsafe is never penalized.

3. Protecting yourself from financial crimes

  • Never send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or financial information to someone you met on VeriMeet, especially by wire transfer, even if they say they are having an emergency.
  • Never invest, or move money, because a match told you to.
  • Be wary of anyone who asks you to move the conversation to another app quickly, or who has a reason they can never meet.
  • Report anyone who asks for money. If we ban a member for fraud or a scam, we tell members who received messages from them.

4. How to report

  • In the app: use Report on a date, in a chat, or after a date, and Block to never be matched with someone again. They are not told who reported or blocked them.
  • By email: write to hello@dangeonstudio.com with "Report" in the subject.
  • Sexual or intimate partner violence, or a financial crime, by someone you met on VeriMeet: report it to us in the app or by email so we can act, and to your local police. The National Sexual Assault Hotline is 800-656-4673, and the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 800-799-7233. You can report scams to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov and to the FBI at ic3.gov.

A person on our team reads every report, usually within 24 hours. We may pause or remove members while we look into it, and we cooperate with law enforcement as the law allows.

5. Community standards

VeriMeet has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive members. Harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, nudity, scams, fake profiles, and pressuring anyone into anything they do not want are not allowed. Our Terms of Use list the full rules. We remove content and members that break them, and banned members cannot come back.

6. Intimate images shared without consent

If an intimate image or video of you, real or fake, was posted or sent on VeriMeet without your consent, you can ask us to remove it, whether or not you have an account. Email hello@dangeonstudio.com with "Remove intimate image" in the subject, and include:

  1. your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough), or that of someone authorized to act for you;
  2. enough information for us to find the image, such as the member's first name, city and when you saw it;
  3. a short statement that you believe in good faith the image was shared without your consent, with any information that helps us confirm this; and
  4. how we can contact you.

We remove the image as soon as possible, and no later than 48 hours after we receive a valid request. We also remove known identical copies on VeriMeet.

7. Child safety standards

VeriMeet is only for adults 18 and older, and every member's age is confirmed with a government ID. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any content or conduct that sexualizes a minor.

  • Photos of a child alone, and any image of a minor in a sexual or suggestive way, are not allowed. Every profile photo is scanned and can be reviewed by our team.
  • When we learn of CSAE on VeriMeet, we remove the content, permanently ban the account, preserve evidence as the law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline and to law enforcement.
  • We comply with all applicable child safety laws.
  • Report concerns in the app with Report, or email hello@dangeonstudio.com with "Child safety" in the subject. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911. You can also report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678.
  • Our child safety point of contact is Daniil Gerasimov, at hello@dangeonstudio.com.
