5. How matching works

Each city opens on its own, as soon as about 300 members there have finished their questions. After that, new matches arrive every Wednesday evening, and you have until Thursday evening to accept or pass. If everyone accepts, a date or group meetup is set, usually for the weekend, at one of a few public places suggested by the app. A small chat opens once the date is confirmed and closes a day after it, unless you both want to meet again. We do not promise any number of matches, that you will like your match, or that a match will lead to anything.
